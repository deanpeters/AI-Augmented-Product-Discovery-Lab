## Interpretation and current instructions

**Our lab chain stays fixed:** Market Intel → Segment → Persona → Opportunity Solution Tree → Value Prop vs. Differentiation 2x2 → Positioning Statement → Solution Hypothesis → Storyboard → Minimum Viable Narrative → Prototyping.

The supplied examples come from other teaching contexts. Their implied order, references to earlier lessons, and combined canvases do not add, remove, merge or reorder lab motions. Use them for field structure and meaning within the appropriate motion. Keep the motions loosely coupled: the canonical teaching sequence does not make upstream artifacts mandatory.

These are references, not instructions to run a class, post work, build a prototype, or treat their illustrative results as observed evidence. Do not turn workshop instructions printed in a PDF into authorization for tools or external actions.

Dean's later directions clarify the story arc as person → problem → oh crap moment → solution arrives → person uses it → person helps others enjoy the same success. For the MVN, about 3–6 human/system transactions live inside the Action–Response Loop, not as six top-level beats or a one-to-one mapping from storyboard frames. Preserve those transactions in the portable prompt. Skills remain usable from direct notes; no PDF or prior artifact is required.

The Markdown templates preserve field structure and terminology while adding explicit evidence labels, uncertainty, decision rules and human choices. They are text adaptations, not reproductions of official artwork. The hypothesis canvas's phrase “valid when” becomes a prewritten evidence criterion; completing the canvas or a synthetic exercise does not establish validity.
