## Problem and positioning context

Jordan needs a defensible way to act before equipment fails; conflicting warnings and access constraints may prevent it. Before the Bang pairs an inspectable intervention case with available plant-window context rather than adding another opaque priority list.

**Preserve supplied prototype hypothesis verbatim:** “Surface the highest-risk equipment so managers can intervene before failure.”

The task tests whether the person can make a credible early-action decision. It cannot establish predictive model accuracy, prevented failures or product-to-market fit.

## If / for / then / because

**If we** surface developing equipment risk with source evidence, contradictions, uncertainty and a feasible next action **for** maintenance managers at A1 sites, **then we will** see them identify a justified verification/intervention and its timing before the fictional breakdown point.

Mechanism hypothesis: useful warning lead time becomes action only when the manager can trust its basis, challenge it and arrange a practical window. “Highest risk” depends on consequence, persistent comparable trend and evidence quality; a stale severity flag alone is insufficient.

Riskiest assumption: a usable predictive signal can change an actionable maintenance decision earlier than the cheaper process or existing service. Do not mistake explanation preference for actual action or budget.

## Prewritten protocol and decision rule

Rubric fields: (1) justified priority, (2) correct treatment of contradiction/staleness, (3) verification/action conditional on evidence, (4) named owner and workable timing, (5) retained uncertainty/revisit condition. Pre-review cases and acceptable answers with a reliability practitioner; standards/task outcomes are not supplied by AI.

- **Pursue another bounded field test**, not product build, only if T1 criteria hold AND T2 documents actionable lead time and a credible incremental value/payer path. Obtain comparative supplier evidence before superiority claims.
- **Revise toward coordination/process** if risk is understood but window/authority prevents action, or S3 matches performance cheaply.
- **Punt this segment/bet** if no pre-event signal or feasible intervention exists, economics stay negative, or an installed supplier solves the job with lower total burden.
- **Pause real-world application** for any unsafe certainty or attempt to act without applicable plant procedures; repair the prototype test. No operational guidance is validated here.

A five-person purposive test is an early signal, not statistical validation. Historical replay cannot prove causally avoided hours. A later prospective, expert-reviewed observational test would be needed to assess real prediction/action outcomes.
