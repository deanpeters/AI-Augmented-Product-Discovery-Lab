## Slide 15 - Solution Hypothesis

### Step
07

### Subtitle
What do we believe will change, and what would make us revise or stop?

### Artifact
**Solution Hypothesis**

### IF / THEN
**If we** flag emerging equipment risks and explain why they matter,  
**for** maintenance managers,  
**then we will** help them decide what to inspect or intervene on before failure.

### 2 Tiny Acts of Discovery
1. Review recent failures to identify causes
2. **Simulate equipment failures using synthetic IIoT data**

### Quantitative + Qualitative Metrics
1. Reduce % of failures via interventions
2. Increase manager confidence in identifying emerging issues

### Revise or Stop If
**Predictions fail to change maintenance outcomes.**

### Key point
Test whether predictive insight actually changes maintenance behavior and outcomes before investing further.

### Rule
**Write the rule before the result exists.**

### Status
**NOT RUN**
