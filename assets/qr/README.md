# Repository QR codes

Target: https://github.com/deanpeters/AI-Augmented-Product-Discovery-Lab

- [Productside QR PNG](repo-qr-productside.png): black modules on white, supplied green Productside logomark centered.
- [Productside QR SVG](repo-qr-productside.svg): vector version for resizing and print.
- [Plain QR PNG](repo-qr-plain.png) and [plain QR SVG](repo-qr-plain.svg): fallback without logo.

Generated locally with high error correction and a four-module white quiet zone. Both PNGs decode to the exact repository URL at native size, 512px and 320px widths using an independent ZXing decoder. Keep the white margin intact. Check with a phone from the audience's viewing distance after placing it on a slide.

The supplied logo is Productside property, used with Dean's previously confirmed sharing permission. QR files encode a direct GitHub link without a shortener or tracking service.

For the workshop board, see [Mural QR codes](MURAL.md).
